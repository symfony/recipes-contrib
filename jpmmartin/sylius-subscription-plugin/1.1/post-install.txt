  * <fg=blue>Four steps no recipe can take, in this order:</>

    1. Let your order item carry the subscription plan: <comment>src/Entity/Order/OrderItem.php</comment>
       implements <comment>JpmMartin\SyliusSubscriptionPlugin\Entity\SubscriptionPlanAwareInterface</comment>
       and uses <comment>JpmMartin\SyliusSubscriptionPlugin\Entity\SubscriptionPlanAwareTrait</comment>.
       Until it does, no product is offered by subscription, and the admin says so.

    2. <comment>bin/console doctrine:migrations:migrate</comment>

    3. Name the payment methods that charge renewals in
       <comment>config/packages/jpm_martin_sylius_subscription.yaml</comment>: nobody can subscribe until you do.

    4. Run <comment>bin/console jpm-martin:subscription:process-cycles</comment> on a schedule, as often as
       renewals are to be charged.
