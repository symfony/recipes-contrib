       <comment>security:</>
       <comment>    role_hierarchy:</>
       <comment>        ROLE_ADMINISTRATION_ACCESS: [ROLE_AI_TRANSLATION_MANAGE]</>

    4. AI generation runs asynchronously: keep a worker on the engine transport.

       <comment>bin/console messenger:consume cyllene_ai_translation</>
